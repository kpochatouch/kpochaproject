## Who should use this guide?

This guide is for service providers who want to join Kpocha Touch as a professional. It explains how to register, create a public profile, manage bookings, get paid, and build a successful business on the platform.

## What is a Kpocha Touch professional?

A professional is any verified individual, creator, freelancer, technician, artisan, vendor, consultant, business operator, educator, influencer, or service provider who uses the platform to offer services, build visibility, communicate with users, publish content, or manage customer relationships.

This may include:

- Local businesses and entrepreneurs
- Freelancers and digital professionals
- Technicians and artisans
- Creators and influencers
- Event and media professionals
- Health and wellness providers
- Logistics and support operators
- Educators and consultants
- Beauty and lifestyle specialists
- Other verified service providers

## Becoming a professional

**Step 1: Prerequisites**

- Must be at least 18 years old
- Must have completed your client profile (full name, phone, state/LGA, address)
- Must have a valid Nigerian bank account (10-digit account number)

**Step 2: The Multi-Step Application**

The "Become a Pro" form is divided into sections:

1. **Identity Information**

   - Full name (as it appears on your ID)
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - State and Local Government Area (LGA)
   - Residential address
   - A clear profile photo (of yourself, verified via face liveness)

2. **Services**

   - List each service you offer with a clear name (e.g., "Hair braiding", "Haircut", "Threading")
   - Set pricing for each service in Nigerian Naira (₦)
   - Optional: set promotional pricing for introductory offers

3. **Business Details**

   - Business name (if different from your personal name)
   - Shop/workspace location (state and LGA)
   - Photos of your shop/workspace (outside and inside photos)
   - Service area coverage (nationwide or specific states)

4. **Availability**

   - Days of operation (Monday-Sunday)
   - Opening and closing times
   - States you cover (if not nationwide)

5. **Bank Account (for Payouts)**

   - Bank name (selected from Nigerian bank list)
   - 10-digit account number
   - Account holder name (must match your bank records)
   - This information is verified with your bank before payouts are enabled

6. **Portfolio**
   - Photos of your work (strongly recommended for credibility)
   - Up to 10 high-quality images showcasing services

**Step 3: Face Verification**

- After filling all sections, you must complete a face liveness check
- This is an anti-fraud measure that verifies you are a real person
- The check takes 1-2 minutes using your device camera
- If verification fails, you can retry immediately

**Step 4: Agreement Acceptance**

- Review and accept the Professional Service Agreement
- Review and accept the Privacy Policy
- Both must be accepted to proceed

**Step 5: Submission & Approval**

- Your application is submitted to Kpocha Touch for review
- You can check your status in the app (Status: "submitted")
- Approval typically takes 1-3 business days
- You will receive a notification once approved
- You can accept bookings immediately after approval

## Creating and managing your pro profile

Your public professional profile is what clients see when they browse the platform.

**What appears on your profile:**

- Profile photo (verified via face check)
- Professional/business name
- Services offered with prices
- Ratings and review summary (once clients leave reviews)
- Portfolio images (if you uploaded them)
- Location and service area
- Hours of operation
- Number of jobs completed
- Verified status badge (if applicable)

**Updating your profile:**

- Go to **Settings > Professional** to edit your information
- Changes take effect immediately
- Update prices, availability, and gallery images anytime
- Add new services or remove old ones
- Change your service area coverage without re-approval

**Important:** Keep your profile information current and accurate. Clients make booking decisions based on what they see. High-quality photos and competitive pricing attract more bookings.

## Listing services

Services are the core of your business on Kpocha Touch.

**Adding a service:**

1. Go to **Settings > Professional > Services**
2. Enter the service name (e.g., "Relaxer Weave", "Eyebrow threading")
3. Set the standard price in ₦ (Nigerian Naira)
4. Optionally set a promotional price for discounts
5. Save—the service is immediately available to clients

**Pricing tips:**

- Price should reflect your expertise and market rates in your location
- Clients can see all service prices before booking
- You can update prices anytime (for future bookings only; already-booked prices don't change)
- Competitive pricing and positive reviews attract more bookings

**Managing services:**

- Edit any service detail (name, price, promo price)
- Remove services you no longer offer
- Add new services as your business grows

## Posting content and building visibility

Beyond bookings, Kpocha Touch lets you publish posts to build your brand and engage with the community.

**Creating posts:**

1. Tap **Compose** to write a new post
2. Add text (up to 500 words) describing your services, tips, promotions, or updates
3. Optionally attach:
   - One image (up to 10 MB)
   - One video (up to 80 MB, max 120 seconds)
4. Tap **Post** — it goes live on the platform

**Post ideas for professionals:**

- Before/after photos of your work
- "Tips & tricks" related to your service
- Promotional announcements (e.g., "New clients get 10% off!")
- Business updates (e.g., "Now accepting bookings on Saturdays")
- Thank you posts for completed jobs
- FAQ or educational content about your service
