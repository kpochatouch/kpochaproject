# Kpocha Touch Professional Guide

## Who should use this guide?

This guide is for service providers who want to join Kpocha Touch as a professional. It explains how to register, create a public profile, manage bookings, and get paid.

## What is a Kpocha Touch professional?

A professional is a verified service provider who offers work through the platform. This can include:

- Beauty and personal care specialists.
- Health and wellness providers.
- Home service technicians.
- Event support and service professionals.
- Other verified service experts.

## Becoming a professional

1. Sign up or log in to your account.
2. Open the “Become a Pro” or professional registration page.
3. Submit your personal details and service profile.
4. Upload required verification documents as requested.
5. Wait for approval from the platform.

## Creating your pro profile

Your professional profile should include:

- A clear professional name or business name.
- Profile photo and gallery images of your work.
- Service categories and pricing.
- Location or service area details.
- Contact preferences for clients.
- Verification status if available.

## Listing services

- Add the services you offer with clear prices.
- Use service names that clients will understand.
- Update availability and location when your schedule changes.
- Keep service descriptions accurate and professional.

## Managing bookings

- Review new booking requests promptly.
- Accept or decline bookings through the app.
- Communicate with clients if there are questions.
- Honour bookings on time and at the agreed location.
- If you must cancel, do so through the platform and provide notice.

## Payments and earnings

- Earnings are calculated after platform commission.
- Payouts are sent according to your configured payout method.
- Use the Wallet to receive credit or process refunds.
- Do not ask clients for unauthorized direct cash payments.

## Professional conduct

Kpocha Touch expects professionals to:

- Deliver services professionally and respectfully.
- Maintain hygiene and appropriate equipment.
- Keep client details confidential.
- Follow the platform’s cancellation and quality policies.
- Respect safety and compliance rules.

## Profile visibility and privacy

- Public profile information is visible to clients and browse users.
- Private contact details are kept hidden and shared only when appropriate.
- Sensitive data is protected by the platform.

## Support and resources

- Use the in-app help or support contact for questions.
- Read the Legal &amp; Privacy pages for obligations and rights.
- Keep your profile and service listings current to attract more bookings.
