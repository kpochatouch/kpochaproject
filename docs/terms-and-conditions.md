1. Overview

These Terms & Conditions govern the use of the Kpocha Touch platform, a marketplace connecting clients and professionals for on-demand and scheduled services. By using the platform, you agree to these terms.

2. Definitions

- "Client": a user who requests and pays for services.
- "Professional": a service provider listed on the platform.
- "Booking": an agreed appointment or service order between Client and Professional.
- "Holdings wallet" or "pending balance": funds held by the platform after a booking is marked completed.
- "Available balance": funds that have cleared platform holds and are withdrawable by the Professional.

3. Payments, Holds and Payouts

3.1 Payment collection

- All booking payments are collected at booking time and held by the platform until booking completion and applicable holding periods expire.

  3.2 Completion and holdings wallet

- A booking is considered completed when the Client taps **Completed** in the app. Upon completion, the booking amount moves to the Professional's holdings wallet (pending balance).
- If the Client does not mark the booking completed within 2 hours after the expected end time, the Professional will receive an in-app notification to end the booking and provide a reason. The platform may review the case and apply the rules below.

  3.3 Holding period and auto-release

- Holdings wallet funds are subject to a default holding period of 3 calendar days for fraud checks and dispute resolution. After this period, pending funds automatically move to the Professional's available balance.

  3.4 Scheduled cashout (7-day cashout)

- The platform operates a scheduled cashout cycle where bookings that remain for the scheduled cashout at 7 days after job completion are distributed under a 75% (Professional) / 25% (Platform) split of the booking amount.

  3.5 Early release and maintenance fee

- Professionals may request early release from the holdings wallet to available balance after the 3-day hold if they do not wish to wait for the scheduled 7-day cashout. Early release is subject to a maintenance fee of 3% of the amount released. The fee is taken by the platform at the time of release.

  3.6 Withdrawals

- Withdrawals to bank require a verified bank account and confirmation using the Professional's 4-digit withdrawal PIN. Standard bank transfers timelines apply; instant options may be available subject to fees.

4. Cancellation and refunds

4.1 Client cancellations

- If a Client cancels before the Professional accepts the booking, the Client receives a full refund to their Wallet.
- If a Client cancels after the Professional has accepted the booking, a 3% cancellation fee applies (split 1.5% to the platform and 1.5% to the Professional). The remaining amount is refunded to the Client's Wallet.

  4.2 Professional cancellations & no-shows

- If a Professional cancels after accepting, the Client receives a full refund and the Professional must provide a cancellation reason. Professional cancellations may affect Professional standing and are subject to platform review.
- If a Professional fails to show for a booking, the Client is entitled to a full refund.

  4.3 Failed bookings

- Bookings that fail due to system issues or other non-performance events are refunded in full to the Client's Wallet.

5. Reviews and disputes

- Clients may leave ratings and reviews after completion. Reviews may impact visibility and standing of Professionals.
- Disputes should be reported through the in-app support flow. The platform will investigate and may reverse funds or take other corrective actions.

6. Security — Withdrawal PIN

- Professionals must set a 4-digit withdrawal PIN to authorize bank withdrawals. The PIN is stored securely in hashed form. PIN resets require identity verification.

7. Notifications and completion disputes

- If a Client does not mark a booking as completed within 2 hours after the expected end time and refuses or does not respond, the Professional will receive an in-app notification prompting them to end the booking and supply a reason for ending. The platform may then adjudicate and apply the rules above.
