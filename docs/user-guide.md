## What is Kpocha Touch?

Kpocha Touch is a marketplace for professional services in Nigeria. It helps clients discover and book verified professionals across categories like beauty, wellness, health, home services, events, and more.

The platform is built for:

- **Clients** who want trusted professionals for bookings and home service visits
- **Professionals** who want to offer services, grow their reputation, and manage bookings in one place

## Main features

- **Search and browse** professionals by service, state, or local government area (LGA)
- **View public professional profiles** with service lists, ratings, and gallery images
- **Book services securely** through the platform with full transparency
- **Pay safely** with supported payment providers and Wallet credit
- **Manage bookings**, chat with professionals, and leave reviews
- **Access legal**, privacy, and support information inside the app
- **Post content** and follow professionals to build your network
- **Make voice and video calls** with professionals directly in the app

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** by signing up with your email and phone number
2. **Complete your profile** with your name and contact details
3. **Complete face verification** (anti-fraud check, 1-2 minutes)
4. **Browse professionals** and select a service that fits your needs
5. **Book and pay** through the secure platform

## How to book a service

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Find a professional** — Search, browse, or tap their profile from a post
2. **Select the service** — Choose which service you want and confirm the price
3. **Confirm your details** — Verify your address where the professional will visit (or confirm if online service)
4. **Create the booking** — The app may offer:
   - **ASAP** (immediately available) — Professional accepts and arrives same day
   - **Scheduled** — Pick a date/time you prefer; professional accepts and confirms
5. **Choose payment method:**
   - **Wallet** — Pay with your Wallet balance (must have sufficient credits)
   - **Card** — Pay with debit card via Paystack (secure payment processor)
6. **Confirm payment** — Complete payment; booking is now confirmed
7. **Wait for professional** — You can chat and share location in the app
8. **Service is completed** — Mark as complete when done, or professional marks it
9. **Leave a review** — Rate and review the professional (optional but encouraged)

**Payment happens upfront:**

- Full payment is taken at booking time
- Funds are held securely until the job is completed
- If professional doesn't show, you get a full refund
- If you cancel within 24 hours, you get a full refund
- Cancellations within 24 hours of booking time are subject to cancellation fees (varies)

**Cancel a booking:**

- Tap your booking → **Cancel Booking**
- Explain why you're cancelling
- Refund processes within 7 working days to your original payment method or Wallet

Cancellation specifics:

- If a client cancels _before_ the professional accepts the booking: full refund to the client's Wallet.
- If a client cancels _after_ the professional has accepted the booking: a 3% cancellation fee is charged on the booking amount (split 1.5% to the platform and 1.5% to the booked professional), and the remainder is refunded to the client's Wallet.
- If a booking fails (system error or cannot be fulfilled): full refund to the client's Wallet.
- If a professional cancels a booking after accepting, the client receives a full refund and the professional must provide a cancellation reason.

## Managing bookings

**View your bookings:**

- Go to **Bookings** to see all your current and past bookings
- Filter by status: Scheduled, Accepted, Completed, Cancelled

**While booking is scheduled (waiting for professional to accept):**

- Chat with the professional if you have questions
- Share your location so they know where to go
- Cancel if plans change (cancellation policy applies)

**After professional accepts (status: Accepted):**

- Use **Chat** to communicate about timing, specific requests, or questions
- Make **Voice/Video calls** directly in the app
- Track location if they're on their way (share live location)
- Rate their communication and professionalism

**After job is completed:**

- Mark booking **Completed** in the app. When you tap **Completed**, the booking payment moves to the professional's holdings wallet (pending balance) held by the platform for security and dispute protection.
- **Leave a review** (1-5 stars + comment) about your experience
- Funds in the professional's holdings wallet are subject to the platform's hold and cashout rules (see **Wallet and payments** below).

**Dispute a booking:**

- If something went wrong, tap **Report Issue** in booking details
- Explain what happened and attach photos if relevant
- Kpocha Touch support will investigate
- Refunds may be issued based on investigation

## Wallet and payments

**What is the Wallet?**

- A digital account inside Kpocha Touch
- Used to store credits for faster booking
- Receives refunds automatically
- Tracks all your transactions

**Topping up your Wallet:**

1. Go to **Wallet** → **Add Credit**
2. Enter amount in ₦ (Nigerian Naira)
3. Choose payment method:
   - **Paystack Card** (debit/credit card)
   - Other integrated payment methods
4. Complete payment securely
5. Credits appear instantly in your Wallet
