# Kpocha Touch User Guide

## What is Kpocha Touch?

Kpocha Touch is a marketplace for professional services in Nigeria. It helps clients discover and book verified professionals across categories like beauty, wellness, health, home services, events, and more.

The platform is built for:

- Clients who want trusted professionals for bookings and home service visits.
- Professionals who want to offer services, grow their reputation, and manage bookings in one place.

## Main features

- Search and browse professionals by service, state, or local government area (LGA).
- View public professional profiles, service lists, ratings, and gallery images.
- Book services securely through the platform.
- Pay safely with supported payment providers and Wallet credit.
- Manage bookings, chat with professionals, and leave reviews.
- Access legal, privacy, and support information inside the app.

## Getting started

1. Open the Kpocha Touch website or mobile app.
2. Create an account by signing up with your email and phone number.
3. Complete your profile with your name and contact details.
4. Browse professionals and select a service that fits your needs.

## How to search and browse

- Use the search box to find services by name.
- Filter professionals by service category, location, and availability.
- Tap a professional’s card to view their public profile and service offerings.

## Viewing a professional profile

A public professional profile typically includes:

- Name and verified status.
- Profile photo and gallery images.
- Services offered with prices.
- Ratings, reviews, and completed jobs.
- Location or service area.

## How to book a service

1. Open the professional profile or service listing.
2. Choose the service you want to book.
3. Select the preferred date, time, and location.
4. Confirm your booking details.
5. Complete payment using the supported checkout flow.

> The platform is designed to protect you. Do not pay professionals directly outside the app unless specifically authorized by Kpocha Touch.

## Managing bookings

- Use your account dashboard to view upcoming bookings.
- Track booking status, appointment details, and payment progress.
- If you need to cancel or reschedule, follow the in-app cancellation policy.
- Leave a review after the service is completed.

## Wallet and payments

- Your Wallet is a digital transaction account inside Kpocha Touch.
- You can use Wallet credit to book services faster.
- Refunds and adjustments are processed through the Wallet or your payment method.
- If a professional cancels or does not show up, the platform may issue a refund according to policy.

## Safety and trust

Kpocha Touch is built with trust in mind:

- Professionals may be verified based on identity and documentation.
- Sensitive client information is only shared when needed to fulfil bookings.
- The platform monitors service quality and address complaints.
- Direct, unauthorized cash payments to professionals are discouraged.

## Support and help

If you need help:

- Check the FAQ in `docs/faq.md`.
- Use the in-app support or contact channel.
- Review the legal and privacy sections inside the app.

## Useful pages in the app

- **Home** — Start browsing services and professionals.
- **Browse** — Search professionals and services.
- **Profile** — Manage your account and profile settings.
- **Wallet** — Check your balance and transaction history.
- **Bookings** — Track active and past bookings.
- **Legal** — Read the platform’s terms, privacy policy, and professional agreement.
